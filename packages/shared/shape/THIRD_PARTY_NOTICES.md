# Third-party notices: Shape's icons and logos

`stamp-data.ts` (generated by `scripts/gen-shape-stamps.ts`) bundles drawings from two projects, so `plannotator shape update` can draw an icon or a logo a board names. They are stored in the board as plain SVG; nothing is fetched when a board is shown.
