# Plans home: approval

Approved: 2026-11-16, by the owner, in the Plans Inbox thread.
Artifact: home-3.html (this folder), byte for byte as shown to the owner.

## The owner's words
- On home-3: "Yes, looking really good. The only thing missing is a simpler like list view for people who prefer like a list view of things."
- Earlier, on the direction: "Simplicity is proving best, still lacks a bit of life though"; on motion: "don't change life. Life is good."; "I like how the card opens into the space."
- On what the home is for: "a place I want to come home to to view all plans in progress, where they're at, ... how many artifacts they have, what's coming alive, where my agents are working".

## The state approved
- "What do you want to plan?" and one filled button over the dither field.
- "Now": large cards. Then every other plan, grouped by project, in ONE card size.
- A card: a readable preview (about three quarters of the card) on a quiet tinted ground for its kind; the title; one grey line ("Your move", the agent's mark + "<agent> is working", or else a date); a small unread dot.
- The preview is the real top of the plan in its own type, cut off by the card's edge; or the plan's main file when that is an HTML page, an image or data. No AI and no judgment in making it.
- Stack rule: documents are paper and always show one sheet behind; pages, images and data never do. It does not encode versions.
- Motion (four, and only these): an agent's change landing in the preview; the agent's mark breathing while it works; a new version settling in, getting the dot and re-sorting "Now"; sheets fanning slightly on hover. Opening: the preview grows into the plan page. Idle page: nothing moves. Reduced motion: nothing moves.
- The open plan is the existing plan page. No large header face.

## Decided on the way
- Rejected: review-queue homes (r1-r4, p1/p2), the newspaper home with version trails ("too noisy, feels like slop"), "most telling block" faces as the default, a large face as the open plan's header ("Looks terrible"), a drifting dither field and draw-in on arrival (cut as decoration).
- Versions are not shown on the home.

## Still to come under this approval

- Fixes the owner was told about: no title twice on a document card; titles may take two lines.

## Out of scope

## Addendum, 2026-11-16: list view and two card fixes (home-4.html)

- List row, 44 px: a 36x27 thumbnail of the card's own face on its kind ground, the unread dot, the title, the project (only in "Now"), and at the right the one grey line ("Your move", agent mark + "<agent> is working", or the date). Same grouping. No version marks, excerpts or counts. Fills only for hover and focus.

Status: sent to the owner as the screen to be built; build proceeds unless they change it.

## Addendum 2, 2026-11-16: the home at scale, the name, pinning (home-5.html) — APPROVED TO BUILD
The owner on the 48-plan list: "I get pissed if I immediately have to sc[roll] ... if it's always changing on me ... Industry had figured this out. We gotta find it." After research (ten references) home-5.html was shown, and to "Build the home this way?" the owner answered: "Yes, and add pinning".
home-5.html = home-4.html plus:
- Home is "Now" (never cut; only active items) + "Recent" (8 rows in the list view, one row of cards in the cards view) + an "All plans" link. It fits one screen with 48 plans.
- The sidebar picks the scope: clicking a project shows only that project ("Now" first), with a way back to Home. "All plans" is a separate view grouped by project (newest 5 or one row of cards per project, then "Show N more"; a project's name folds its group; folds remembered).
- "Search plans" at the top right beside the view switch, across all titles. No new shortcuts.
- Nothing moves under the person: when something arrives while they are looking (pointer on the page, or scrolled/typed in the last 4 s), its preview, unread dot and fact update in place but its position waits; a quiet "N updates · Show" pill beside "Now" settles everything on click. Not looking: it settles by itself. Reduced motion: a cut.
- Naming: the sidebar item and page are "Spaces"; "On this computer" at the top right; "What do you want to plan?" and "Start a plan" stay. Nothing on screen implies another person.
- Pinning (asked for in the approving answer; not in the mock): a space can be pinned; pinned spaces show in the sidebar. To be designed in the build, following the Inbox's existing pinned-threads pattern, and shown to the owner.
Decisions recorded the same evening: the panel is Spaces ("Yes"); a plan is what an agent puts into a space; `plannotator plans`, plan review and "plan" as the agent's act keep their names.
