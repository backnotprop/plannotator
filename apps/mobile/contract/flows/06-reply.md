# 6. Replying, with photos and voice

The composer is one floating field at the bottom of a thread: plus, the text, the microphone, Send. It rises with the keyboard; Send is always above it.

- **Plus**: Photo library, Take a photo, Scan a document, Files, Skills, To another session. A photo or file shows as a tile above the text with its upload progress; a tile can be removed until Send.
- **Microphone**: dictation into the field, on the phone. Press and hold records; the words appear as they are heard.
- **@**: typing it lists the agents in the thread; "#" lists tags.
- **Recipient**: the line above the field names who gets the reply when it is not the obvious one; tap to change it. A session that ended offers "Leave it for the next agent" or another live session, as the window does.
- **Draft**: words typed here are saved to the computer as the thread's reply draft, the same one the window shows, and kept on the phone if the computer is away. The list row shows the unsent mark.
- **Send**: one press. The message appears at once with "Sending", then the delivery line the computer reports.

From outside the app: the share sheet in Photos, Safari, Files or any app offers "Plannotator". It shows the recent threads and "New message"; the shared picture, link or text lands in that thread's draft, ready to send.

Differs from the window: no paste-a-picture into the text (iOS cannot), so the plus and the share sheet do that job. Voice uses the phone's own speech recognition and needs no speech key on the computer.
