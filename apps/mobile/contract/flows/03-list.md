# 3. The list

On screen, top to bottom: the computer's name with its state dot; the pencil; the large title "Inbox"; the filter pills; then the sections in the Inbox's order with the Inbox's names: Stopped on you, Holding up work, Waiting on you, New since you looked, Sent, Quiet. Starred threads sit above the sections as a row of pills, Snoozed folds at the foot, as in the window. Empty sections are not drawn.

A row: the agent's mark, the thread's name (bold while unread), the time, "agent · project" (and the computer when more than one is shown), the latest message in grey (the person's own as "You: …"), and at most one tag on the right: "2 questions", "Will send", "Not delivered", "Working", "Starting".

What the person can do:

- **Tap** a row: the thread.
- **Swipe right**: Mark read (a full swipe does it); on a read row, Mark unread. **Swipe left**: Archive and Snooze. These are the window's directions.
- **Press and hold**: the row menu with everything else: Star, Snooze, Priority, Tag, Mark unread, Archive, Delete, Copy link, Share.
- **Pull down**: read again.
- **Filter pills**: All, Unread, Starred, Pull requests, a project; "Show only" for an agent comes from the Agents tab.
- After Mark read, Archive, Delete or Snooze, an Undo banner stays 10 seconds. Mark read that sets questions aside warns first, with the window's words.

The list holds still, as the window's does: while the person reads the list, an agent's news does not move rows under the thumb. A pill says "3 new" and a tap applies them. Coming back from a thread moves only that thread's row.

Differs from the window: no sidebar; the count on the app icon is the number of rows that wait on the person (the window's tab count); Starred order is changed with press, hold and drag.
