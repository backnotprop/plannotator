# 12. Offline, and a computer that is asleep

Three states, shown by the dot beside the computer's name and one line under the title:

| State | The line | What works |
|---|---|---|
| Here (direct or through the relay) | none | Everything |
| The computer is asleep or off; the relay holds its mail | "Work Mac is asleep. You are reading what this phone has. Two answers will send when it wakes." | Reading what the phone has, including questions that arrived before it slept. Answers, replies, marks and stars are held and sent in order when it wakes. Run, a secret, Start, uploads over the relay's size and search of old threads wait |
| The phone has no network | "No connection. Showing what this phone has." | Reading. Writes are held on the phone, then go when the network is back |

A held write shows where the person made it: the pick stays picked, the reply shows "Will send when Work Mac is back", and the row carries "Will send". Each held line has Cancel until it has left.

When the computer returns, the phone reads again and the computer's answer wins. If an answer was held and the question was replaced meanwhile, the answer is not applied; the thread says "This question was replaced while your answer waited" beside the new question, and the person's words are kept in the draft. Nothing a person typed is ever dropped silently.
