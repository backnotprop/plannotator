# Check, then fix

Do both parts, fix what fails, and check again. Two or three passes is normal.

## 1. Fact check

Check against the source, not your memory. Best: give a fresh agent only the source and your page, and ask it to list every claim the source does not support, with the message that settles each. Otherwise do it yourself, slowly:

- **Re-read the last five messages.** Is anything on the page out of date, reversed or renamed? A later correction wins.
- **Quotes are copied, not retyped:** character for character, with their "maybe" and hedges. A paraphrase gets no quotation marks. Never quote words nobody wrote.
- **Keep the source's certainty.** "idk if it's useful" is not "not useful".
- **A promise is not a result.** "Fixing the header in the same PR" means fixed only when a later message says so. Date each beat by the message that reports it done.
- **A tick needs a report that it was done.** "Would probably" is not a check.
- **A qualifier needs a rule still in force.** No "regular users" if the activity rule was cut.
- **Nothing from outside the source.** Numbers, names, timings, component names and card titles from code, commits, screenshots or your own knowledge stay off both views and the note. If the person asked for outside facts, label them ("from the code").
- **How it works adds no rules** the source did not state.

## 2. Look

Render and look at every image, not the HTML: 1440 × 900 light and dark (`?static&theme=light`, `?static&theme=dark`, each also with `&view=tech`), a quarter-size thumbnail, and a 390 × 844 phone. Playwright, a browser tool or Chrome's `--headless --screenshot` all work.

**Newcomer test.** From the picture and note alone (better: ask a fresh agent who sees only the screenshot), can someone new say what it is about, where it stands, what is settled, what is open and whose call, and what is next?

**Glance test.** At thumbnail size the hero and the accent are found in three seconds. The page fits one 1440 × 900 screen.

**Must not appear:** a metaphor, abstract geometry or a flow as the picture; wobble, gradients, emoji, clip-art; hands, faces, figures, cursors; dashboard parts (side borders, badges, pills, legends, identical rows, caption stacks); identifiers on the picture, blank screens, invented content; the accent on more than two things; a status beside its own contradiction; more than 100 words on the picture; text over a line or cut off.

**Must appear:** real things with real content; the biggest decision with a tick; every open question with whose call; next, dashed; solid lines only where true today; a clear hero; How it works answering when, how and what; claim, picture, note and How it works agreeing; dark mode as readable as light.
